# Pet Leveling: EXP Progression System

## Rewards Per Action
- **Pet Button**: 5 EXP (1-hour cooldown)
- **Feed Button**: 10 EXP per use (costs 5 Food, no cooldown)

---

## EXP Requirements Per Level

| Level | Total EXP Needed | Next Level Cost | Cumulative Days* | Notes |
|-------|-----------------|-----------------|-----------------|-------|
| 1 | 0 | 100 | 0 | Egg just hatched |
| 2 | 100 | 150 | 0.5-1 | Quick early progress |
| 3 | 250 | 200 | 1-2 | Still ramping |
| 4 | 450 | 275 | 2-3 | Mid-game begins |
| 5 | 725 | 350 | 3-4 | First "milestone" |
| 6 | 1,075 | 450 | 5-6 | Grind increases |
| 7 | 1,525 | 575 | 7-8 | Upper mid-game |
| 8 | 2,100 | 700 | 9-11 | **Serious grind** |
| 9 | 2,800 | 875 | 12-14 | **Very hard** |
| 10 | 3,675 | 1,100 | 15-18 | **Endgame boss** |
| 11 | 4,775 | ∞ | 19-22 | Max level (prestige point) |

*Assumes: 5 pet button clicks/day (25 EXP) + 10 feed actions/day (100 EXP) = ~125 EXP/day

---

## Progression Philosophy

**Early Game (L1-3)**: Fast, rewarding. Players feel accomplishment quickly.

**Mid Game (L4-6)**: Moderate grind. Players invest but still see progress.

**Late Game (L7-8)**: Real commitment. Dedicated players pull ahead.

**Endgame (L9-10)**: **Prestige territory.** Only hardcore guildees reach this. Level 10 is a guild status symbol.

---

## Long-term Gameplay

**Daily engagement**: 
- Pet button (1/day): 5 EXP
- Feed (flexible): 10 EXP × N uses = 10N EXP
- Total: 5 + 10N EXP/day

**Casual player** (feeds 5x/day): ~55 EXP/day = Takes **~67 days** to max

**Active player** (feeds 20x/day): ~205 EXP/day = Takes **~23 days** to max

**Hardcore player** (farms food, feeds constantly): Could reach L10 in **2-3 weeks**

---

## Evolution Stages

Based on level milestones:
- **Juvenile**: L1-L4 (Small, cute model)
- **Adult**: L5-L9 (Medium, capable model)
- **Boss**: L10+ (Large, powerful model)

---

## Feedback Loop

✅ **Rewarding**: Every action (pet button, feed) gives visible EXP progress

✅ **Long-term**: Takes weeks to max, not hours

✅ **Gating**: Food scarcity controls feeding pace (you mentioned they'd need to earn food)

✅ **Social**: Level 10 pets become guild status symbols

✅ **Sustainable**: Guildees log in daily to click pet button + feed
